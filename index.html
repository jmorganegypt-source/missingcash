Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this service really free? ▾
Yes! The basic search is completely free with no sign-up required. We only charge for premium guides and specialist recovery services.
How much unclaimed money is there in Australia? ▾
Over $16 billion in lost and unclaimed money is held by the Australian government and financial institutions. This includes super, shares, bank accounts, and more. About 1 in 3 Australians has unclaimed money waiting for them.
Is my personal information secure? ▾
Absolutely. We use bank-grade encryption and never store or sell your personal details. We only use your name to perform the search against government databases.
How long does it take to get my money back? ▾
The search is instant. Once you submit a claim, most institutions process it within 10–28 business days. Super funds can sometimes take 60–90 days if they require ID verification.
Can I search for a deceased family member's lost money? ▾
Yes. As an executor or administrator of an estate, you can search for and claim unclaimed money on behalf of a deceased person. Our Premium Guide covers the estate process in detail.
What is the Premium Guide? ▾
The MissingCash Premium Guide is a 50+ page PDF that walks you step-by-step through the entire claims process for every type of unclaimed money — super, shares, bank accounts, and more. It includes pre-written templates and scripts for dealing with institutions.
Can you recover lost cryptocurrency? ▾
Our Crypto Recovery Service helps with lost wallet access, forgotten seed phrases, and exchange account recovery. The $29.90 fee covers a full assessment. Recovery success depends on the type of loss — our specialists will advise you honestly.
